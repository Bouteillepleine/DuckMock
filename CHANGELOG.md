# Changelog

## 1.4.3

Launcher icon carries the duck alongside the pin.

## 1.4.2

Module updates are now offered in KernelSU, Magisk and APatch.

## 1.4.1

Readme trimmed to what the app actually does.

## 1.4.0

The floating joystick can be resized and closed, and stands aside while the manager is open.
The module finds the manager again after it is reinstalled, instead of going quiet until the
next reboot.

## 1.3.0

Routes: record a walk, import and export GPX, and play it back with `Stop`, `Loop` or
`Bounce` at the last point. The joystick moved to its own tab.

## 1.2.0

A floating joystick that walks the fake position at a chosen pace, carrying a real bearing
and speed.
